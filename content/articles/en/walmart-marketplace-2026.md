Brands that treat Walmart Marketplace as "Amazon, a few years behind" consistently underperform on it. The platforms rhyme, but they do not repeat.

## Where the playbooks diverge

Walmart rewards operational reliability — fulfillment speed, in-stock rates, and price trust — more visibly than Amazon does. Advertising matters, but it sits on top of an operational foundation that the marketplace scores you on first.

1. **Fulfillment** is a ranking input, not just a logistics cost.
2. **Price competitiveness** is enforced more aggressively and more publicly.
3. **Catalog quality** gates discoverability before ad spend ever enters the picture.

## What to build first

Get the operational base right before scaling traffic. A brand with clean catalog data, reliable fulfillment, and defensible pricing can scale advertising efficiently. A brand without it is paying to amplify its own weaknesses.

The opportunity in 2026 is real, but it belongs to operators, not just advertisers.
